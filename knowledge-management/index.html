Knowledge Management

Tools • Systems • Independence

Knowledge management is the practice of capturing, organizing, and leveraging information for better thinking, learning, and decision-making. This field encompasses both the theoretical foundations of information systems and the practical tools we use to extend our cognitive capabilities.

My work in this area focuses on building independent, open-ended software solutions that respect user agency and promote deep engagement with ideas. Rather than closed proprietary systems, I advocate for tools that enhance human thought without constraining it.

The goal is to create systems that:

  • Respect privacy and user autonomy
  • Enable nonlinear exploration of ideas
  • Facilitate connection-making across domains
  • Support both individual and collaborative knowledge work
